1. Overview

Hours is an independently developed, free iOS utility that allows users to create a list of cities and view their time zones side by side. It has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, paid features, or advertising.

The App’s core features include:

  • adding cities from a bundled city database;
  • supplementing local city search with Apple MapKit results;
  • optionally detecting the user’s current city through location services;
  • displaying weather information and calculated sunrise and sunset times for selected cities;
  • creating calendar events by explicit user action;
  • creating reminders by explicit user action;
  • scheduling local notifications on the device;
  • renaming saved cities with custom labels;
  • reordering or deleting saved cities, with the first city serving as Primary;
  • viewing cities on the World Map, in Basic, Compact, or Cards views, and in supported widgets;
  • switching between 12-hour and 24-hour time formats;
  • switching between system, light, and dark appearance modes;
  • adjusting time offsets using a rotary dial interface; and
  • opening the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use within the App.

Core time zone conversion and local catalog search work without internet access. Remote search, reverse geocoding, new weather data, and hosted legal pages require a connection. Map visualization and sunrise and sunset calculations use available city coordinates locally.

2. Information the App Processes

The App does not create user accounts and does not collect personal profiles. However, the App processes certain categories of information in order to function.

a. Saved City Data

The App stores the following information locally on your device for the cities you save:

  • city identifier;
  • city name;
  • time zone identifier;
  • optional custom city display name;
  • geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude), where available; and
  • order of saved cities, which determines the Primary city.

b. User Interface Preferences

The App stores your selected interface preferences locally on your device, including:

  • time format preference (system / 12-hour / 24-hour);
  • appearance preference (system / light / dark);
  • city view preference (Basic / Compact / Cards); and
  • whether onboarding has been completed.

c. Search Queries

While you search, the text you enter may be sent to Apple MapKit to supplement local results, including when some local matches exist. Hours uses Apple search suggestions and place resolution to identify additional cities and their geographic information.

d. Location Information

If you grant location access, Hours requests individual device location fixes and sends them to Apple for reverse geocoding to identify a city or locality and its time zone. Requests can occur when Hours becomes active and when relevant screens appear. Hours does not continuously track your movements or request background location updates. Location may be precise or approximate depending on your iOS permission settings and the location information available. If permission is denied, current-city detection is unavailable, but you can still search for and add cities manually.

A saved current-location city may use catalog coordinates, Apple-resolved coordinates, or a device location fix as a fallback. Manually selected cities use their catalog or search-result coordinates, not your device location. Available coordinates are retained with the saved city and used for map markers, weather, and local solar calculations.

e. Weather Data

Hours requests weather data from Open-Meteo using the selected city’s latitude, longitude, time zone, and parameters specifying the weather variables and time range. These requests do not include your name, custom city labels, or account information. For a saved current-location city, the coordinates may be location-derived, including a device location fix retained as a fallback. Open-Meteo also receives connection information such as your IP address.

f. Calendar, Reminders, and Notifications

When you choose a Calendar or Reminders action, Hours requests the relevant permission if needed. It uses system calendar or list information to prepare a new calendar event in Apple’s editor or create a new reminder with your chosen title, time, and related city information. Hours requests write-only Calendar access on supported iOS versions and full Reminders access for its reminder-creation feature. These features are optional, and Hours does not upload calendar or reminder contents to a developer-operated backend. Items may synchronize through your configured Calendar or Reminders accounts. Notifications scheduled by Hours are local notifications.

g. Support Email Information

If you choose “Contact Me,” Hours prepares an editable email in the system mail composer or your mail app. That draft may include technical information such as:

  • app version and build;
  • iOS version;
  • device model;
  • device name;
  • hardware model identifier (not a unique device identifier);
  • locale;
  • system time zone;
  • time format preference; and
  • appearance preference.

Hours does not automatically send this message to the Developer. You can edit or remove its contents before sending. Your mail provider may handle or synchronize drafts according to your mail settings. If you send the email, the Developer receives your email address, message, and any included technical information to handle your request.

3. How Information Is Stored

Saved city records and preferences are stored locally. This is separate from the network processing described in Section 4.

The App uses the following storage mechanisms:

a. UserDefaults

The App uses UserDefaults to store:

  • time format and appearance preferences;
  • city view preference; and
  • onboarding completion.

b. Local JSON File Storage

The App stores saved city data, including available coordinates, as a local JSON file within the App’s Application Support directory on the device.

c. Local Widget Storage

Hours shares an ordered copy of saved city identifiers, original and custom names, and time zone identifiers with HoursWidgetExtension through local App Group storage. This shared copy does not include coordinate fields or weather responses. App Group storage is shared storage on the device, not a cloud account. The widget calculates its timelines locally; iOS controls its refresh schedule.

d. Temporary Weather Storage

Hours keeps up to 16 validated weather responses in memory and reuses fresh responses for up to 15 minutes to reduce repeat requests. Responses are not saved to disk and are cleared when the app process terminates. The 15-minute period controls freshness, not a scheduled deletion time. Saved cities and preferences are stored separately and remain available between app sessions.

Hours does not provide user accounts or its own cloud synchronization or remote database for saved cities and preferences. Device backups and synchronization by system apps or configured accounts are governed by your device and account settings.

4. How Information Is Shared

The App does not sell, rent, or otherwise share user data with third parties for marketing or commercial profiling purposes.

However, certain information may be transmitted in the following limited cases:

a. Apple MapKit Services

When you use MapKit-powered search or location-based features, the following may be transmitted to Apple:

  • search queries entered by you; and
  • current device location, only if you have granted location permission.

Apple processes these requests under its own privacy practices. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

b. Weather Data Services

Weather requests send the coordinates and parameters described in Section 2 to Open-Meteo. Its published privacy notice describes technical logging, including logs that may contain geographic coordinates, and states that those logs are deleted after 90 days. This is the provider’s stated practice, separate from Hours’ local cache. See Open-Meteo’s Terms and Privacy notice.

c. Legal Page Display

When you open the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use inside the App, the App loads those pages from: https://sergy.xyz

Hours adds the visual theme preference (light or dark) as a query parameter. The website and its hosting infrastructure also receive ordinary connection and request information, such as your IP address and the requested page. The system web view may retain website data under its normal behavior. These requests are separate from local city storage.

d. User-Initiated Support Email

If you send a support email, its contents and included technical information pass through your email provider and the Developer’s email service. Email handling, including draft synchronization, depends on the services and account settings involved.
